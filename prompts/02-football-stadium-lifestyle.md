# Football Stadium Lifestyle

A source-image prompt for a candid lifestyle moment inside a crowded football
stadium, framed with the visual language of a live sports broadcast.

## Overview

- **Model:** Wan 3.0 workflow
- **Category:** Sports / Lifestyle
- **Workflow:** Image prompt / source frame
- **Style:** Photorealistic live sports broadcast

## Image Prompt

```text
Ultra-realistic sports broadcast still of a glamorous woman sitting in a packed football stadium crowd during a night match. She wears a dark brown sleeveless high-neck satin top and black square earrings, with shoulder-length light brown and blonde hair styled in soft waves.

She is casually drinking from a tall blue aluminum can while holding a half-eaten cheeseburger in her other hand. Around her are fans in bright yellow and blue football jerseys and scarves, creating strong team-color contrast.

The scene feels candid and cinematic, captured mid-game from a television broadcast camera angle with shallow depth of field. Include realistic stadium seating, a crowded audience atmosphere, broadcast overlay graphics in the top-left showing a live football score and match timer, and a sports-network-style watermark in the top-right.

Natural arena lighting, detailed skin texture, sharp focus on the woman, a slightly blurred background crowd, an authentic live sports broadcast aesthetic, and a 16:9 composition.
```

## Why This Prompt Works

- Clothing, accessories, food, and pose give the subject a specific candid identity.
- Yellow and blue crowd colors create strong separation from the subject's neutral clothing.
- The broadcast angle and overlay instructions define the intended visual context.
- Shallow depth of field keeps a busy stadium environment readable.

## Tips

- This is a source-image prompt, not a complete motion prompt. Add a separate animation prompt before using it in an Image-to-Video workflow.
- Generated scoreboards and watermarks may contain inaccurate text. Use generic graphics when exact lettering matters.
- For animation, introduce only a few natural actions at a time, such as lowering the can, glancing toward the field, and subtle crowd movement.
