# Makeup Transformation

A two-step beauty workflow that creates a finished makeup reference and then
animates the transition with Wan 3.0 Start & End Frame.

## Overview

- **Model:** Wan 3.0
- **Category:** Transformation / Beauty
- **Workflow:** Image generation + Start & End Frame
- **Style:** Controlled fantasy beauty transformation

## Step 1: Generate the End Frame

Upload the source portrait to an image-generation model and create the finished
makeup look.

```text
Add vibrant fantasy makeup with purple and yellow gradient eyeshadow, dramatic eyeliner, glitter details under the eyes, and small rhinestones arranged decoratively. Keep the lighting, pose, facial identity, hairstyle, clothing, and background consistent with the source image.
```

## Step 2: Animate With Start & End Frame

Use the original portrait as the start frame and the generated makeup portrait
as the end frame.

```text
The woman slowly lifts her head as the light above illuminates her face, creating a subtle overexposed glow. Under the shifting light, the makeup develops smoothly into the finished look. She then lowers her gaze toward the camera with a soft, controlled expression. Preserve her facial identity, hairstyle, clothing, pose, and background throughout the transition.
```

## Why This Prompt Works

- The end frame defines the desired makeup before animation begins.
- A motivated lighting change helps conceal and connect the visual transition.
- The restrained head and eye movement keeps attention on the transformation.
- Explicit continuity instructions reduce unwanted changes outside the makeup.

## Tips

- Keep the start and end portraits closely aligned in pose and framing.
- Avoid changing the background or wardrobe between frames.
- Use a soft lighting transition rather than an abrupt flash if facial detail is important.
