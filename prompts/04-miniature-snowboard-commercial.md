# Miniature Snowboard Commercial

A surreal product-commercial concept with miniature snowboarders riding a
winding track formed by potato chips in a bright mountain landscape.

## Overview

- **Model:** Wan 3.0
- **Category:** Product Commercial / Surreal
- **Workflow:** Image-to-Video
- **Style:** Cinematic macro product advertising

## Prompt

```text
A surreal miniature winter scene in the mountains. A large blue Pringles can lies tipped over in the snow, spilling potato chips that form a winding snowboard track. Tiny snowboarders ride smoothly along the curved chip-and-snow path, carving through the slope. Snow-covered mountains and a clear blue sky fill the background. Bright daylight, a playful fantasy tone, ultra-realistic textures, crisp snow detail, and shallow depth of field.

Primary Motion: Miniature snowboarders glide downhill in smooth arcs, carving gently along the curved track.

Secondary Motion: Loose chip pieces tumble slowly in the snow, fine snow particles kick up behind the boards, and light powder spray follows each turn.

Camera: Cinematic macro shot with a slow tracking follow along the snowboard path. Use a low angle close to the snow surface, gentle parallax as the camera moves forward, and stable, smooth motion.

Style & Lighting: Bright natural winter daylight, clean contrast, vibrant product colors, soft highlights on the snow, realistic shadows, and a whimsical commercial aesthetic.

Constraints: Keep the Pringles can and logo shape stable and readable. Snowboarders remain miniature and proportional. No distortion of the chips, no melting snow, and no object duplication.
```

## Negative Prompt

```text
warped logo, unreadable text, stretched can, broken snowboarders, extra limbs, chaotic motion, shaky camera, flicker, excessive motion blur, glitch artifacts
```

## Why This Prompt Works

- Primary and secondary motion establish a clear hierarchy of action.
- The low macro camera reinforces the miniature scale.
- Physical constraints protect the product, track, and character proportions.
- The negative prompt targets common failures in branded objects and small figures.

## Tips

- Use a clean source image with a readable track and clear separation between riders.
- Replace the named product with an original package design for brand-neutral work.
- Prioritize stable product geometry over complex background motion.
